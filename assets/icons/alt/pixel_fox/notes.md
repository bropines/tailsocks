Pixel fox hiding in a TailSocks sock: the head peeks over the striped cuff, the cream-tipped tail sticks out of the heel. Same pixel mesh background as sock-tail.
Fill-only `<path>` rects on a 3-unit grid, one path per colour, 6 colours; the first path (#0B0A14) is the silhouette = outline, and it also gives the eyes and nose, so keep it under the others.
monochrome: fox mask (cheeks cut away, eyes/nose/inner ears as holes), tail plume, sock with the cuff stripe cut. Source: ../_src/fox-in-sock.py, rebuild with `python3 _src/gen.py fox-in-sock`.
