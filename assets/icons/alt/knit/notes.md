Idea: a swatch of stockinette, the fabric a sock is knitted from: 2×3 V stitches, lavender at the top shading to dusty apricot in the bottom row (the fox, for those who know). Nothing in it says network or VPN.
Converter: 12 filled paths, one per stitch leg (each a polygonised teardrop), all sharing one userSpaceOnUse linearGradient; the background is a single radial-gradient rect.
Monochrome is the same 12 legs in #FFFFFF, with no holes needed.
