Low-poly fox head, 22 flat facets in 5 tones (#FBDCC7, #F2C2A4, #FFB172, #FD610A, #BC4343; eyes/nose #2A2140). The facet edges are drawn as mesh edges (#4D4671, 0.6) and the vertices as mesh nodes, so the fox is a patch of the tailnet mesh. The bright nodes (ear tips, cheeks, chin) are where the background mesh's lines end.
Converter: each facet has a 0.25 stroke in its own colour to hide anti-aliasing hairlines between facets; keep it. The background is triangulated onto the fox's outer vertices, so foreground and background line up only at zero layer offset. A unit or two of parallax drift is harmless. Elements are path/circle/rect only, with no gradients.
